Talk Meter

Steady Bots classroom tool · v0.3

A small window you can park in the corner of the Smartboard. It watches for voices talking over you — not who is talking — and gives you a count at the end of class.

Privacy: No audio is recorded or sent anywhere. Nothing leaves this browser. No names. Only a numeric voice profile and class counts are saved on this device.
